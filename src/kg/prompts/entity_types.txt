FIN_INSTRUMENT:  Any tradable or issued financial security or derivative, such as "Mortgage-Backed Securities" or "Credit Default Swaps" (illustrative, not exhaustive); NOT a private legal or employment contract. Use the most specific applicable type instead when one fits: government debt is GOV_BOND, a commodity (including its futures) is COMMODITY, a currency is CURRENCY, an equity index is INDEX. FIN_INSTRUMENT is the residual — only for instruments with no more-specific listed type.
