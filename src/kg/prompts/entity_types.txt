PERSON:          Macro-relevant person (e.g., Jerome Powell)
SOVEREIGN:       Sovereign nation (e.g., United States)
GPE:             Geopolitical entity (e.g., European Union, Middle East)
CENTRAL_BANK:    Central bank (e.g., Federal Reserve)
GOV_BODY:        Government body (e.g., U.S. Treasury)
REG_BODY:        Regulatory body (e.g., SEC)
COMPANY:         Macro-relevant company (e.g., Saudi Aramco, ExxonMobil, Apple
                 Inc., JPMorgan)
ORG:             Organization (e.g., IMF, OPEC)
US_GICS_SECTOR:  US GICS sector or sector future (e.g., Consumer Staples)
CURRENCY:        Exchange rate or FX future (e.g., Japanese Yen / USD)
COMMODITY:       Commodity or commodity future (e.g., WTI Crude Oil)
INTEREST_RATE:   Interest rate or short-term rate future (e.g., 3-Month SOFR);
                 NOT a value like 3.5%
EQUITY_INDEX:    Equity index or index future (e.g., S&P 500)
GOV_BOND:        Government bond or bond future (e.g., 10-Year US Treasury Note,
                 Euro Bund)
FIN_INSTRUMENT:  A tradable or issued financial security or derivative with no
                 more-specific type above (e.g., Mortgage-Backed Securities,
                 Credit Default Swaps)
ECON_INDICATOR:  Broad macro aggregate (e.g., U.S. CPI Inflation, GDP); NOT a
                 value like 2.4%, and NOT an asset-specific measure (see
                 ASSET_METRIC)
ASSET_METRIC:    A statistic/measure describing a specific asset (e.g., Net
                 Purchases of Gilts, Gold ETF Holdings, Crude Oil Inventories)
CONCEPT:         Concept (e.g., Recession)
EVENT:           Event (e.g., Russia-Ukraine War, Covid-19)
