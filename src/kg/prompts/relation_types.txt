For the directional relations, the sign is the DIRECTION in which the subject
moves the object's level — never whether that move is good or bad:
  CAUSES_RISE_IN — the subject moves the object's level UP. Pushing a quantity
    that is undesirable to have higher is still CAUSES_RISE_IN; the sign tracks
    the direction of the level, not its desirability.
  CAUSES_FALL_IN — the subject moves the object's level DOWN.
  IMPACT — use ONLY when the text leaves the direction genuinely unclear; never as
    a default to avoid committing to a sign.
  RAISES, DECREASES — reserve for an actor that directly and deliberately sets a
    level; use CAUSES_RISE_IN / CAUSES_FALL_IN for indirect effects.
