 - A foreign central bank is the subject (cross-country rate
   spillover does not transfer to this contract).
 - A different reference rate for the same economy is named (e.g.,
   "Fed funds futures" does not name SOFR).
 - One-clause background citations of the central bank, or
   central-bank language cited only to contextualize another
   asset's move rather than as the article's primary/co-equal
   subject.

===POSITIVE RULES===

A rate contract is relevant when the following positive rule holds AND
none of the disqualifiers above apply:

(2) The article substantively discusses the central bank or monetary
    authority that prices this contract — decisions, statements,
    actions, leadership, forward-calendar entries, or market-reaction
    moves in the expected path of policy. The central bank must be a
    primary or co-equal subject.
