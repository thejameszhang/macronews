These rules apply only to equity-volatility indices tracking the
implied volatility of a specific underlying equity benchmark.

 - Do not tag commodity, currency, or bond volatility — only equity
   volatility qualifies.
 - Do not tag if the underlying equity index is discussed without
   volatility-specific language.
 - Do not tag stock-price moves, selloffs, or generic "uncertainty"
   language without volatility-specific terminology.

===POSITIVE RULES===

A volatility index is relevant when the following positive rule holds
AND none of the disqualifiers above apply:

(2) The article explicitly discusses the volatility of the underlying
    equity benchmark using volatility-specific terminology —
    "volatility," "vol," "implied vol," "realized vol," "vol
    premium," "hedging demand," or a direct vol-index reference.
