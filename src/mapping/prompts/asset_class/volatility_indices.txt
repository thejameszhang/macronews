These rules apply to equity-volatility index groups, each tracking the
implied volatility of one or more underlying equity benchmarks.

 - Do not tag commodity, currency, or bond volatility — only equity
   volatility qualifies.
 - Do not tag stock-price moves, single-name options activity, or
   generic "uncertainty" language. Equity-vol-index groups reflect
   index-level implied or realized volatility, not single-stock
   variance or routine descriptions of price movement.

===POSITIVE RULES===

(2) The article substantively discusses the implied or realized
    volatility of an equity benchmark this group tracks.
