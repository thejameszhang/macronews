These rules apply to equity-volatility index groups, each tracking the
implied volatility of one or more underlying equity benchmarks.

 - Do not tag commodity, currency, or bond volatility — only equity
   volatility qualifies.
 - Do not tag if an underlying equity index is discussed without
   volatility-specific language.
 - Do not tag stock-price moves, selloffs, or generic "uncertainty"
   language without volatility-specific terminology.

===POSITIVE RULES===

A volatility index group is relevant when the following positive rule
holds AND none of the disqualifiers above apply:

(2) The article explicitly discusses the volatility of an underlying
    equity benchmark this group tracks, using volatility-specific
    terminology — "volatility," "vol," "implied vol," "realized
    vol," "vol premium," "hedging demand," or a direct vol-index
    reference.
