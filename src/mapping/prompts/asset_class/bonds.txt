--- GOVERNMENT BOND-SPECIFIC RULES ---
Apply general rule (1) NAMED first, then the following.

(2) BOND-MARKET DISCUSSION: The article must directly discuss the bond market — yield moves, Treasury auctions, curve repricing, term-premium, or duration — for the country whose bond this is. Inferential framings (e.g., "driver of bond-market pricing," "affects rate expectations," "has implications for Treasuries," or central-bank policy / rate-path discussion without a named bond-market phenomenon) do NOT count as direct discussion; the article must name the bond-market mechanism it is engaging with.

(3) SOVEREIGN FISCAL OR DEBT SUPPLY: The article discusses a fiscal or debt-related force specific to the country that issues this bond — deficit trajectory, budget dispute, auction dynamics, credit-rating action, or debt-sustainability concerns — AND states a bond-market implication (yield move, supply concern, debt-service cost, term-premium). A fiscal event named only as a driver of another asset's move does not qualify.

NOT RELEVANT:
 - A foreign central bank or foreign bond is the subject.
 - A different tenor or instrument of the same country is named (each contract must be named specifically).
