 - Do not tag if the currency is named only as a reporting, functional,
   pricing-unit, or category-example mention, with no force acting on
   it specifically.
 - Do not tag calendar-style name-drops or peer-list inclusions ("Bank
   X, Bank Y, and Bank Z will announce decisions this week"). Rule (2)
   requires substantive discussion.
 - Do not tag commodity or safe-haven flows inferred from the article's
   topic without the FX link stated in the text.

===POSITIVE RULES===

A currency group is relevant when one of the following positive rules
holds AND none of the disqualifiers above apply:

(2) The article substantively discusses the central bank or monetary
    authority issuing a currency in this group — its decisions,
    statements, actions, intervention, leadership, or the market
    reaction to them.

(3) The article names a country-specific force — tariffs on the
    country, trade-balance or current-account shift, capital-flow
    event, FX intervention, sovereign-debt event, or country-specific
    fiscal action — and a currency in this group is the primary FX
    instrument through which that country is traded.
