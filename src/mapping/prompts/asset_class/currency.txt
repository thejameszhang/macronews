 - Reporting-, functional-, pricing-unit, or category-example
   mentions (named only to illustrate a broader category, with no
   force in the article acting on this specific currency).
 - Calendar-style name-drops or inclusion in a peer list. If the
   central bank or currency appears only in a sentence listing
   multiple central banks or currencies (e.g., "Bank X, Bank Y, and
   Bank Z will announce decisions this week"), Rule 2 is not
   satisfied.
 - Commodity or safe-haven flows inferred from the article's topic
   without the FX link stated in the text.

===POSITIVE RULES===

A currency is relevant when one of the following positive rules holds
AND none of the disqualifiers above apply:

(2) The article substantively discusses the central bank or monetary
    authority issuing this currency — its decisions, statements,
    actions, intervention, leadership, or the market reaction to
    them.

(3) The article names a country-specific force — tariffs on the
    country, trade-balance or current-account shift, capital-flow
    event, FX intervention, sovereign-debt event, or country-specific
    fiscal action — and this currency is the primary FX instrument
    through which that country is traded.
